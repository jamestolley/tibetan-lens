Read the web in the language you're learning

What we believe

🍵

Free forever at the core

Reading help, dictionaries, saved words and review are free for good, with no cap on how many words you can learn. Any paid extras will add to that, never take it away.

🌱

Learn at a healthy pace

Save as many words as you like. New words join your reviews a few at a time, so learning stays steady instead of turning into a pile of overdue cards.

🔒

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. There are no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Nothing leaves your computer unless you ask for it.

🤝

Built with each community

We use openly licensed dictionaries, credit every source and invite speakers and teachers to shape each language. Learners can suggest definitions too.
