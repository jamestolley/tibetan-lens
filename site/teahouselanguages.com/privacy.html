Privacy

Private by design

Effective October 18, 2026

In short: our extensions don't collect, sell or share personal data. They have no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Everything happens in your own browser.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or ads. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. To show Tibetan text correctly, these pages load a font from Google Fonts.

Forms and email

Our feedback, bug report and sign-up forms are Google Forms. Whatever you enter, including an email address if you give one, is stored in a Google Sheet that only the Teahouse team can open. We use it only to improve our extensions, to reply to you and, if you asked for it, to tell you about launches. If you email us, we use your address only to answer. We never sell or share your information. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete anything you've sent at any time.
