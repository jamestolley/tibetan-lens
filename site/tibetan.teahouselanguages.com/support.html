Common questions

Words on a page aren't spaced or underlined

Reload the page after installing or updating the extension. Check that "Extension enabled" is ticked on the extension's Options page. Text inside images, PDFs and some protected pages, such as the Chrome Web Store itself, can't be read by any extension.

Definitions show up when I hover. Can I make them appear only when I click?

Yes. Click the extension icon (or open the Options page) and set "Show translations on" to Click.

Why are only a few new words added to my reviews each day?

You can save as many words as you like. They wait under "New" and join your reviews a few at a time (10 a day by default), so reviews never pile up. Change the number under "Study settings" on the My words page.

How do I back up my words or move them to a new computer?

Open My words → "Backup & import" → "Download backup", then use "Import a file…" on the new computer. Imports also accept CSV files and Anki text exports.

Some Tibetan shows as empty boxes

Your computer is missing a Tibetan font. Install a free one such as Noto Serif Tibetan or Jomolhari, then restart the browser.
