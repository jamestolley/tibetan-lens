Built on generous open work

Teahouse Tibetan exists because scholars, translators and developers chose to share their work openly. Thank you.

Dictionaries and data

  • 84000: Translating the Words of the Buddha, glossary data, used under CC BY 4.0. read.84000.co
  • Jeffrey Hopkins' Tibetan-Sanskrit-English Dictionary, Dharma Drum Buddhist College digital edition, used under CC BY-SA 3.0. glossaries.dila.edu.tw
  • Esukhia botok-data, tokenization word lists, used under CC BY 4.0 or public domain where marked. github.com/Esukhia/botok-data
  • Annotated Corpus of Classical Tibetan (ACTib II), part-of-speech lexicon via Esukhia's bo-pos collection, used under CC BY 4.0. doi:10.5281/zenodo.822537
  • SOAS part-of-speech tagged corpus of Classical Tibetan (SOAS, University of London; AHRC grant AH/J00152X/1), via Esukhia's bo-pos collection, used under CC BY 4.0. doi:10.5281/zenodo.574878

Contribute a dictionary

We only include dictionaries that are openly licensed or shared with the author's permission. If you have a glossary you'd like Tibetan learners to use, please get in touch.
