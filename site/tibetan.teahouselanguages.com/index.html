Free forever, with no limit on how many words you can learn. Works in Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

Everything a reader needs, right on the page

Tibetan is written without spaces between words, so finding where one word ends is half the work. Teahouse does that part for you, then helps you remember what you've learned.

␣

Word spacing

Words are split with a Tibetan word segmenter (botok), so you can see each word at a glance. You can color them by part of speech too.

📖

Instant definitions

Click or hover a word for its meaning, Wylie and pronunciation, drawn from the 84000, Hopkins and other open dictionaries.

🔎

A full dictionary

Look up any word by Tibetan script, Wylie or English, with results from all the bundled dictionaries.

★

Save words, review them

Star words as you read. Flashcard review uses FSRS, the modern spaced-repetition scheduler, so each word comes back just before you'd forget it.

✓

See what you know

Words you know fade into plain text, and words you're learning get a saffron underline, so new words stand out on every page.

།

Translate a sentence

Click the shad at the end of any sentence to open that sentence in Google Translate. You decide what is sent, and when.

How it works

  1. Add it to your browser. No account, no sign-up.
  2. Read as usual. Tibetan on any page is spaced and ready to look up.
  3. Save words you want to learn. A few new ones a day join your reviews, and you can save as many as you like.
  4. Review for a few minutes a day. Words you've mastered drop back, and new ones take their place.

Private by design

Everything happens on your own computer. The dictionaries are part of the extension, your saved words stay in your browser, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you click "Translate this sentence." Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Reading help, the dictionary, saving words and review are free forever, and there's no cap on how many words you can learn. A paid plan with extra features may come later. It will add to the free version, never take away from it.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome and Microsoft Edge, on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS. Firefox support is planned.

Does it work with classical texts?

Yes. The dictionaries include a lot of Buddhist and classical vocabulary (84000, Hopkins), along with general modern words. If a definition is missing or wrong, please suggest a better one.

Can I move my words to another computer?

Yes. On the My words page, open "Backup & import" to download a backup, then import it on the other computer. You can also import word lists from a CSV file or from Anki.

What languages is the interface in?

English, Chinese (simplified and traditional), Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese. Definitions are in English for now.
