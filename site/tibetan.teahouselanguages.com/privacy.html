Privacy policy

Your reading stays yours

Effective October 18, 2026

In short: Teahouse Tibetan does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and it doesn't run a server. Everything it does happens in your own browser.

What the extension does on the pages you visit

To space and define Tibetan words, the extension reads the text of the pages you open, inside your browser. It checks whether a page contains Tibetan. If it does, it looks the words up in dictionaries that are packaged with the extension. Page text is never stored or sent anywhere.

What the extension stores

  • Your settings (for example, hover or click, colors, and which dictionaries are on) are stored with Chrome's storage.sync. If you have turned on Chrome sync, Google syncs these settings between your own devices, as it does for other extension settings.
  • Your saved words, with their definitions, review history and the address of the page where you saved each one, are stored only on your computer with Chrome's storage.local. They are not synced or uploaded. Backups you download are ordinary files that stay wherever you save them.

Removing the extension deletes all of this from your browser. Download a backup first if you want to keep your words.

When anything leaves your computer

Only when you choose it. If you click Open in Google Translate for a sentence, a new tab opens Google Translate with that one sentence. Google's privacy policy applies to that page. Links in the extension, such as dictionary credits, open in your browser like any other link.

Permissions, and why they're needed

  • Read the pages you visit: to find, space and define Tibetan text on any website you read.
  • Storage: to remember your settings and saved words.
  • Active tab: so the popup can refresh or test the page you're looking at.

This website and our feedback forms

This website has no cookies, analytics or ads. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. To show Tibetan text correctly, these pages load a font from Google Fonts.

The feedback, bug report and definition forms are Google Forms. Whatever you enter, including an email address if you choose to give one, is stored in a Google Sheet that only the Teahouse team can open. We use it only to improve Teahouse and to reply to you. If you email us, we use your address only to answer. We never sell or share it. You can ask us to delete anything you've sent at any time.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

The use of information by Teahouse Tibetan complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The extension does not collect user data.

Children

Teahouse Tibetan collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future paid plan needs an account, we'll update this page and its date before the change takes effect, and explain what's different. Features that send data anywhere will always be optional.
